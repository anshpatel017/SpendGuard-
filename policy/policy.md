# Procurement Policy

**Document ID:** SG-PP · **Version:** 1.0 · **Currency:** Indian Rupees (INR, `₹`)

---

## Provenance and status — read this first

This is a **representative organizational procurement policy authored by the SpendGuard team**. It is not the internal policy of any real organization, and it is not an official government document.

It exists because the public procurement datasets used in this project do not ship with the issuing organization's internal policy manual, and the `policy_lookup` tool needs a real document to retrieve over.

Its clauses are **derived from** published Indian public-procurement rules:

| Source | What was drawn from it |
|---|---|
| General Financial Rules (GFR) 2017 | The value-band procurement ladder and the modes of procurement at each band |
| Central Vigilance Commission (CVC) procurement guidance | The prohibition on splitting purchases to avoid sanction limits, and vendor-integrity expectations |
| Government e-Marketplace (GeM) framework | The default-channel expectation for common goods and services |

**Caveat.** Rule references below are indicative of where a clause derives from, not verbatim citations. Clause numbering (`SG-PP-x.y`) is this document's own. Anyone adapting this for real use must verify every figure against the current official text.

## 1. Scope and principles

**SG-PP-1.1 — Application.**
This policy applies to all procurement of goods, services and works by the organization, regardless of funding source, and to every officer who initiates, approves, receives or pays for a purchase.

**SG-PP-1.2 — Core principles.**
All procurement shall be conducted with transparency, fairness, competition, and demonstrable value for money. Every purchase must be traceable to a documented need, an approved budget line, and a named approving authority.

**SG-PP-1.3 — Record retention.**
Complete procurement records — indent, approval, quotations or bids, purchase order, receipt, invoice and payment advice — shall be retained for not less than seven financial years and shall be available for audit at any time.

**SG-PP-1.4 — Default channel.**
Where a required item is available on the Government e-Marketplace or an equivalent approved catalogue, procurement shall ordinarily be made through that channel. Departure requires written justification recorded on file.

## 2. Approval thresholds

**SG-PP-2.1 — Value bands and procurement mode.**
The mode of procurement and the approving authority are determined by the estimated value of the purchase, exclusive of taxes:

| Band | Estimated value | Procurement mode | Approving authority |
|---|---|---|---|
| A | Up to **₹25,000** | Direct purchase without quotation, against a written certificate of reasonableness by the purchasing officer | Purchasing Officer |
| B | Above **₹25,000** up to **₹2,50,000** | Purchase on the recommendation of a Local Purchase Committee of not fewer than three members | Head of Department |
| C | Above **₹2,50,000** up to **₹25,00,000** | Limited Tender Enquiry — written bids invited from not fewer than three eligible suppliers | Procurement Head |
| D | Above **₹25,00,000** | Advertised Tender Enquiry — open public advertisement | Finance Committee |

*Derived from GFR 2017, Rules 154–156, 161 and 162.*

**SG-PP-2.2 — The principal control threshold.**
**₹2,50,000** is the principal control threshold of this policy. At and above this value a purchase leaves the committee route and enters formal competitive tendering. All splitting controls in Section 3 are anchored to this figure.

**SG-PP-2.3 — Threshold is on aggregate requirement.**
The applicable band is determined by the **total estimated value of the requirement**, not by the value of any individual purchase order, invoice or instalment through which it is met.

**SG-PP-2.4 — Certificate of reasonableness.**
For Band A purchases, the purchasing officer shall record in writing that the item is of the requisite quality and specification, and that the price is reasonable against prevailing market rates.

**SG-PP-2.5 — Single-source procurement.**
Procurement from a single source, without competition, is permitted only where the item is proprietary, where standardization with existing equipment is demonstrably required, or in a recorded emergency. Written justification signed by the approving authority is mandatory and is subject to audit.

**SG-PP-2.6 — No retrospective approval.**
Approval shall be obtained before a commitment is made to a supplier. Approval sought after the fact is a breach of this policy and shall be reported irrespective of the value involved.

## 3. Prohibition of split purchases

**SG-PP-3.1 — Splitting is prohibited.**
No requirement shall be divided into smaller purchases, orders, lots or instalments for the purpose of bringing the value of each below a threshold in SG-PP-2.1, and thereby avoiding the procurement mode or the approving authority that the aggregate value requires.

*Derived from CVC guidance on splitting of demands to avoid sanction of the competent authority.*

**SG-PP-3.2 — Indicators of splitting.**
The following pattern shall be treated as a prima facie indicator of splitting and requires written explanation from the initiating officer:

Two or more purchase orders that, taken together, satisfy **all** of the following:

- placed with the **same supplier**
- initiated by the **same officer or department**
- placed within a window of **fourteen days**
- each individually **below ₹2,50,000**
- with an **aggregate value at or above ₹2,50,000**

**SG-PP-3.3 — Shorter windows attract higher scrutiny.**
The fourteen-day window in SG-PP-3.2 is the outer limit. Groups falling within three or seven days carry a stronger presumption of deliberate splitting and shall be escalated to the Procurement Head.

**SG-PP-3.4 — Aggregation of similar items.**
Requirements for the same or substantially similar goods, arising in the same department within a financial quarter, shall be aggregated and procured together unless there is a recorded operational reason not to.

**SG-PP-3.5 — Legitimate exceptions.**
The following are not splitting, provided the reason is recorded on file at the time:

- genuinely separate requirements arising independently, for different purposes
- scheduled instalments under a single approved contract, where the contract was approved at its full aggregate value
- emergency purchases made under a recorded emergency authorization
- purchases where an aggregate requirement could not reasonably have been foreseen

**SG-PP-3.6 — Consequence.**
Where splitting is established, the purchase shall be regularized by the authority competent for the aggregate value, and the matter shall be referred for disciplinary consideration.

## 4. Duplicate payment prevention and recovery

**SG-PP-4.1 — Unique invoice registration.**
Every supplier invoice shall be registered against a unique invoice number per supplier per financial year. The accounting system shall reject a second registration of the same supplier and invoice number.

**SG-PP-4.2 — Pre-payment duplicate check.**
Before any disbursement, the payables function shall check for an existing payment to the same supplier with the same or a closely similar amount within the preceding **ninety days**. Any match shall be cleared in writing before payment is released.

**SG-PP-4.3 — Supplier identity discipline.**
A supplier shall be registered once in the vendor master. Variant spellings, differing legal suffixes, and changes of trading style shall be maintained as aliases against the existing supplier record, never as new supplier records.

**SG-PP-4.4 — Indicators of duplicate payment.**
The following combination shall be treated as a suspected duplicate and withheld from payment pending review:

- same supplier, allowing for variant spelling of the registered name
- identical amount, or amounts differing by less than **0.5%**
- transaction dates within **fourteen days** of each other
- invoice references that are identical, or that differ only in formatting, punctuation or sequence separators

**SG-PP-4.5 — Recovery.**
Where a duplicate payment is confirmed, recovery shall be initiated immediately by set-off against the supplier's next payment or by direct demand. The incident shall be recorded in the duplicate-payment register with the amount, date, cause and recovery status.

**SG-PP-4.6 — Segregation of duties.**
The officer who initiates a purchase shall not be the officer who approves the corresponding payment.

## 5. Vendor onboarding and integrity

**SG-PP-5.1 — Registration before award.**
No purchase order shall be placed with a supplier that is not registered in the vendor master. Registration requires, at minimum: legal name and constitution, address, PAN, GSTIN where applicable, bank account details, and a signed declaration of no conflict of interest.

**SG-PP-5.2 — Verification of bank details.**
Supplier bank details, and any subsequent change to them, shall be verified independently of the request itself and approved by an officer not involved in initiating the purchase.

**SG-PP-5.3 — New vendor, high value.**
A purchase at or above **₹2,50,000** placed with a supplier registered within the preceding **ninety days** requires additional written approval from the Procurement Head, recording why an established supplier was not used.

**SG-PP-5.4 — Conflict of interest.**
No officer shall participate in any procurement decision involving a supplier in which that officer, or a relative of that officer, holds an interest. Such interests shall be declared in writing on appointment and on any change.

**SG-PP-5.5 — Indicators of vendor irregularity.**
The following patterns require review of the supplier's transaction history:

- multiple registered suppliers sharing a PAN, bank account, address, telephone number or authorized signatory
- invoice amounts concentrated on round figures inconsistent with the goods supplied
- a first-digit distribution of invoice amounts that departs materially from the distribution expected of naturally occurring transaction values
- invoice volume concentrated at month end or financial-year end without a corresponding operational reason
- a supplier that receives a disproportionate share of one officer's or one department's purchases

**SG-PP-5.6 — Suspension.**
A supplier under active investigation may be suspended from further awards by the Procurement Head pending outcome. Suspension shall be recorded in the vendor master with the reason and date.

## 6. Price reasonableness

**SG-PP-6.1 — Duty to establish reasonableness.**
No purchase shall be approved without a recorded basis for concluding that the price is reasonable. The basis may be competitive bids, a published catalogue or rate contract, prevailing market rates, or the organization's own recent purchase history for the same item.

**SG-PP-6.2 — Comparison against internal history.**
Where the organization has purchased the same or a substantially similar item within the preceding twelve months, the proposed unit price shall be compared against that history and any material increase shall be explained in writing.

**SG-PP-6.3 — Indicator of price irregularity.**
A unit price materially above the established norm for its item category, absent a recorded change in specification, quantity, urgency or market conditions, shall be treated as an indicator of price inflation and referred for review before payment.

**SG-PP-6.4 — Quantity discipline.**
Unit price shall be assessed together with quantity. A high unit price on a small quantity, and an unusually large quantity at a marginally elevated unit price, are both matters requiring explanation.

**SG-PP-6.5 — Rate contracts.**
Where a rate contract exists for an item, procurement above the contracted rate requires written justification approved by the Procurement Head.

---

## 7. Period-end procurement discipline

**SG-PP-7.1 — Year-end purchases.**
Purchases initiated in the final month of the financial year that are not part of the approved annual procurement plan require written justification from the Head of Department.

**SG-PP-7.2 — Budget exhaustion is not a justification.**
The availability of unspent budget is not, by itself, a valid reason to procure. Purchases made solely to exhaust a budget allocation are a breach of this policy.

**SG-PP-7.3 — Receipt before payment.**
Payment shall not be released against goods or services not yet received, except under an advance expressly permitted by the contract and secured appropriately.

---

## 8. Audit, reporting and review

**SG-PP-8.1 — Right of audit.**
The internal audit function may examine any procurement transaction at any time and shall be given unrestricted access to all associated records.

**SG-PP-8.2 — Automated monitoring.**
The organization may operate automated controls that examine procurement transactions for the indicators described in this policy. An automated flag is an indicator for review, not a finding of wrongdoing, and shall be evaluated by a responsible officer before any action is taken.

**SG-PP-8.3 — Human decision.**
No adverse action shall be taken against a supplier or an officer on the basis of an automated flag alone. Every flag requires review, and the reviewing officer's decision and reasons shall be recorded.

**SG-PP-8.4 — Reporting.**
Suspected breaches shall be reported to the Procurement Head and, where fraud is suspected, to the audit committee. Reports made in good faith are protected.

**SG-PP-8.5 — Review of this policy.**
This policy shall be reviewed annually, and the threshold figures in SG-PP-2.1 shall be revised whenever the underlying statutory limits change.

## Note for retrieval

This document is the corpus for the `policy_lookup` tool. Two properties matter:

1. **Each `SG-PP-x.y` clause is self-contained** and is intended to be retrieved and cited on its own, without surrounding context.
2. **Chunk on clause boundaries, not on a fixed character count.** Splitting a clause in half produces retrieved text that reads as authoritative but is incomplete, which is exactly the failure mode the Verifier exists to catch.

The tool returns both the clause identifier and its text, so an audit note cites `SG-PP-3.2` rather than quoting policy text with no traceable source.
